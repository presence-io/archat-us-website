Frequently asked questions

What is Romi?

Romi is an AR emoji and social discovery app. It brings emojis to life with augmented reality, lets you discover creative people, and turns your profile into a personal vibe canvas.

How much does Romi cost?

Romi is free to download. Optional Premium subscriptions and Bronze coins are available as in-app purchases through the App Store. See the full pricing.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your Apple ID settings. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Full details are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Which devices are supported?

Romi is available for iPhone on the App Store. Make sure your device is running a recent version of iOS for the best AR experience.

Where are my creations stored?

Content you save stays on your device unless you choose to share it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Romi is intended for users aged 18 and older.

How do I manage camera and microphone permissions?

You can grant or revoke camera and microphone access at any time in your device's Settings. Some AR features require these permissions to work.
