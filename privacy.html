Privacy Policy

How Philomuse Limited handles your information in Romi.

Effective date: June 2025 · Applies to the Romi mobile application.

Romi ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Philomuse Limited, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the Romi mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device information — device model, operating system, app version, and IP address.
  • Usage data — including gesture interactions and voice input, which are processed locally on your device.
  • Content you create — captures and creations saved in the App, which are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Crash logs — diagnostic information used for troubleshooting and stability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To enable gesture- and voice-driven visual effects.
  • To operate and provide the App's features.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve the experience.
  • To maintain the stability and performance of the App.

3. Storage and Sharing

  • We do not gather personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or contacts.
  • Content you save stays on your device unless you choose to share it.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We may use third-party analytics services; these do not access personally identifiable information.

4. Your Rights

You can delete saved content at any time, and you can revoke camera or microphone permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Protection (Hong Kong PDPO)

Philomuse Limited is based in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, and we handle personal data in accordance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong ("PDPO"). Consistent with the PDPO's data protection principles, we collect only the data needed to operate the App, use it for the purposes described in this policy, keep it no longer than necessary, and take reasonable steps to protect it. Where you are located in a jurisdiction with additional data-protection laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR), we honor the rights available to you under those laws, including the rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Support page.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will provide notice within the App.
