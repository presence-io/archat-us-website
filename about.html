Our mission

We believe self-expression should feel effortless and fun. Romi combines real-time AR effects, a creative discovery feed, and richly customizable profiles so that anyone can turn an ordinary moment into visual magic — and connect with people who share their vibe.

What we build

  • AR emoji camera — emojis that come alive and react around you in real time.
  • Creative discovery — meet new people through expressive, emoji-rich profiles.
  • Moments feed — scroll genuine, in-the-moment posts from the community.
  • Vibe canvas profiles — style a profile that captures exactly who you are.
